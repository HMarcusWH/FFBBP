# Publication scope

This public repository is restricted to reviewed mathematical exposition, standalone abstract examples, their formal proofs and public-facing documentation. Material is assessed before inclusion; an archive reference does not itself authorize transfer.

Do not publish credentials, personal information, restricted implementation material, private evidence records, or third-party assets without redistribution rights. Retain original archives separately. Do not represent publication as scientific validation, operational authorization, or closure of unresolved obligations.

For mathematical claims, distinguish a proposed statement, a proof candidate, a kernel-checked theorem, an inspected axiom dependency record, and independent review of the intended statement. Report failures and unresolved questions explicitly. A compilation success is not sufficient if a proof uses unapproved assumptions or incomplete proofs.

A project-wide license remains to be selected by the owner. No license grant is implied by public visibility alone.
